# CloudPulse Design System Specification

> Downloaded from https://designmd.ai/chef/cloudpulse

## Overview
CloudPulse is a design system crafted for cloud infrastructure monitoring, financial tracking, and calm dashboards that need to convey complex data without overwhelming the operator. The philosophy centers on calm confidence — generous whitespace, soft rounded surfaces, and a sky-to-violet gradient language that evokes reliability and uptime. Panels support both light and dark modes.

## Colors
- **Primary** (`#0EA5E9`): Primary actions, active nav items, healthy status indicators (Sky Blue)
- **Secondary** (`#8B5CF6`): Accent highlights, secondary actions, gradient accents (Violet)
- **Tertiary** (`#14B8A6`): Positive metrics, savings badges, throughput markers (Teal)
- **Background Light** (`#F8FAFC`): App-level canvas (Light mode default)
- **Surface Light** (`#FFFFFF`): Cards, modals, popovers
- **Background Dark** (`#0B0F19`): Deep slate dark canvas
- **Surface Dark** (`#111827`): Dark card surfaces
- **Surface Dark Alt** (`#1E293B`): Dark inputs and alternate surfaces
- **Border Light** (`#E2E8F0`): Clean subtle borders
- **Border Dark** (`#334155`): Dark subtle borders
- **Success** (`#22C55E`): Positive transactions, paid debts, green alerts
- **Warning** (`#EAB308`): Approaching budget limit, upcoming bill
- **Error / Danger** (`#EF4444`): Overspending, mandatory unpaid items
- **Info** (`#0EA5E9`): General status notifications

## Typography
- **Headlines & Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Body, Labels, Controls**: DM Sans
- **Monospace, Currency, Numbers**: Roboto Mono

## Corner Radius
- **Small (4px)**: Tags, small badges, compact chips
- **Medium (8px)**: Inputs, buttons, dropdowns, filter chips
- **Large (12px)**: Cards, panels, modals
- **XL (20px)**: Hero cards, onboarding dialogs, feature banners
- **Full (9999px)**: Avatars, status dots, pill badges

## Elevation
- **Subtle**: Soft diffused ambient shadow for cards
- **Glow (Primary)**: `20px` glow `#0EA5E9` at 15% opacity on active cards/widgets
